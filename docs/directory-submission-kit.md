## 通用提交文案（直接粘贴）

**Name:** CopyBoss

**Tagline（短标语，任选其一）：**
- One click, 45 ready-to-post copies
- AI copywriting for every social platform
- Never stare at a blank caption again

**Short description（≤250 字符）:**
CopyBoss generates ready-to-post social media copy in one click. Pick your platforms (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, YouTube) and tones, and get 45 tailored copies with matching hashtags — not generic AI text. 1 credit = 1 platform × 1 tone = 3 copies. Free to try daily.

**Long description:**
CopyBoss is an AI copywriting tool built for creators and small businesses who post every day. Instead of one generic AI answer, you get platform-native copy: Instagram captions with hashtags, TikTok hooks, LinkedIn posts, X posts, and YouTube titles/descriptions — each written in the style that actually works on that platform.

How it works:
1. Describe your product or topic (one sentence is enough)
2. Pick platforms and tones (e.g. Instagram + TikTok, casual + professional)
3. Get tailored copies for every combination — ready to copy and post

Pricing: Free 1 credit/day (3 copies), Pro from $9.99/mo (10 credits/day), pay-as-you-go top-ups from $1.

**Categories / tags:** AI Writing, Copywriting, Social Media Marketing, Content Creation, Marketing

**Website:** https://mtboss.cn

I run a few small online projects and every post meant rewriting the same thing 5 times for Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X and YouTube. Generic ChatGPT output doesn't work — each platform has its own style.

So I built CopyBoss: type your topic once, tick the platforms and tones you want, and it generates platform-native copy for every combination (with hashtags). One click = up to 45 copies.

It's free to try (3 copies/day, no card needed). Would love feedback from anyone who posts daily: https://mtboss.cn
