## 三、Maker Comment（制作者评论，决定转化的核心，发完帖子立刻发）

> 这是 PH 文化的关键：不像广告，像讲故事。下面的版本是"独立开发者 + 解决自己痛点"的叙事，转化率最高的类型。

```
Hey PH! 👋 I'm the maker of CopyBoss.

The backstory: I've been helping small businesses with their social media, and I kept seeing the same problem — writing one good post takes 30+ minutes, and business owners just don't have that time. So their accounts go silent.

Most AI writing tools give you generic output that works "everywhere" but lands nowhere. Every platform is actually different: a TikTok hook is not a LinkedIn post is not an Instagram caption.

So CopyBoss is platform-aware instead of platform-agnostic:
→ Pick Instagram / TikTok / LinkedIn / X / YouTube
→ Pick a tone (Friendly / Professional / Witty / Story)
→ Get 10 distinct posts written for how each platform actually works — hooks for TikTok, structured takes for LinkedIn, etc.

Everything is editable and copy-paste ready — one click to copy all, or export as a .txt file. Your history is saved so you can come back to drafts.

Fresh in time for launch: 🎙 Brand Voice — save your product info and tone preferences once, and every generation automatically sounds like you. Plus output in 7 languages (English, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Japanese) and platform-tuned hashtags built in.

Pricing: 1 credit = 1 platform × 1 tone (3 copies). Free gives you 1 credit/day, Pro is $9.99/mo (10 credits/day = 30 copies) or $99/yr (15 credits/day = 45 copies). No credit card needed to test it.

I built this solo and I'm here all day — ask me anything, and I'd genuinely love feedback on the copy quality. Roast my outputs if they deserve it 🔥
```

## 六、发布当天拉票节奏（决定成败的 8 小时）

PH 算法核心是**发布后前几小时的 upvote 数量和评论质量**。节奏：

| 时间（发布后） | 动作 |
|---|---|
| 0-1h | 发 Maker Comment；发给家人朋友请注册+upvote+留评（一句话真实评论 > 空洞赞） |
| 1-3h | 发 X/Twitter（带上帖子链接+短视频/GIF）；发到你的微信朋友圈和社群 |
| 3-5h | 回复每一条评论（PH 算法权重很高）；Reddit r/SideProject 同步发 |
| 6-8h | 午间美东用户上线，再推一波社交分享；更新评论（如"200 visitors in 3 hours, thanks!"） |
| 次日 | 把最终排名截图发出来："#4 Product of the Day"（用户看重这个荣誉） |

**注意**：PH 明令禁止拉票群互刷，真实朋友正常推荐没问题，别买票。
